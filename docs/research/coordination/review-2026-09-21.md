8. [major] §3.4 same-device liveness · §4.6 row 1 · §11 row 2 · §2.2 clock-jump claim — `startedAt ≥ bootAt` with `bootAt = wallNow − os.uptime()` is wall-clock derived: a forward clock step Δ (NTP after sleep, VM snapshot restore, manual set) larger than the process's age-since-boot makes a LIVE process read `stale-reused-pid`; `takeRunLock` then REPLACES its `run.lock` and a second engine opens the same run dir on the same device — the one-writer invariant of `state.json` is broken. Today's `lockIsLive` is host + pid only (`src/session/lock.ts:57-59`) and `acquireRunLock` swaps any `'stale'` verdict (`lock.ts:117-125`), so this is a newly introduced false-dead path.
   Evidence: §3.4 "`startedAt ≥ bootAt` (else the pid was reused after a reboot — `os.uptime()` gives `bootAt`)"; §11 row 2 "`takeRunLock` replaces with `run.lock pid N predates boot; replaced`"; §2.2 "a local clock jump can never flip a peer live/stale". Arithmetic: process starts at uptime u1 (startedAt = W0+u1); clock stepped +Δ; computed bootAt = W0+Δ; Δ > u1 ⇒ startedAt < bootAt.
   Fix: use a boot identity, not wall arithmetic: Linux `/proc/sys/kernel/random/boot_id`, macOS `sysctl -n kern.bootsessionuuid` (one sync spawn, only on the rare "pid alive but heuristic says pre-boot" path). Record `bootId` in `run.lock` (additive) and the heartbeat; `stale-reused-pid` requires `bootId !== mine`. Without a bootId (old lock) a pid that answers `kill(pid,0)` is NEVER auto-replaced — warn and require `sessions unlock`. `lock.test.ts`: startedAt = boot+10 min, reader clock stepped +15 min, pid alive → still live.

10. [major] §4.3 step 2 `check` · §4.6 row 1 · §5.1 `expiresAt` · §11 row 6 — `expiresAt` on leases and messages is a writer wall-clock instant evaluated against the reader's wall clock. With skew the fence and control plane misfire: a reader ≥ 10 min ahead treats a live, renewing peer's lease (ttl 10 min) as expired and drops it from `check()`; a reader > 10 min ahead silently discards every `pause`/`end`/`abort`/`steer` message (10-min expiry) with no ack; a reader behind extends dead leases.
