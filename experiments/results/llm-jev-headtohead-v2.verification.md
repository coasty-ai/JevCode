## Discrepancies to fix

- **Median rounding is inconsistent within the claim.** Under the stated nearest-rank convention the pooled medians are 62.9659 / 390.8436 / 111.4462 s. The claim floors the first ("62") and rounds the second ("391"); the same table rounds the mean (76.687 → 77) and the sum (13,449.8 → 13,450). Consistent rounding reads **63 / 391 / 111 s** (ratio 0.161×), consistent flooring **62 / 390 / 111** (0.159×). Pick one.
- **"0 discordant losses" needs the qualifier "on pass".** On correct-by-verdict v2 loses 2 discordant pairs to both baselines (`detect_cycle`, `stats`).
- **"frozen tree 066816f" must not cover the 19/28 arm.** `jev-off` ran from `baseline-clean` @ 214bf55 on jevcode 0.2.0, ~8 h earlier. `experiments/results/llm-jev-headtohead-v2.md:3` says "Every bench ran from the frozen worktree …" in the same sentence that introduces the baseline; rewrite as "Both arms run here (`llm-jev`, `jev-off-tuned`) ran from …; the `jev-off` baseline is the committed 214bf55 measurement."
- **"same limits" needs a generator carve-out.** `jev-off` carries no `generation` block (maxTokens 4096, no deadline, no `reasoning {effort: low}`, no `lengthHandling`) against v2's 3000 and tuned's 1500; bench-level `--spend-cap` also differs (0.6/1.0/1.2 vs 0.8/1.2/1.5, never binding).
- **Ladder `units` is mislabelled `equivalent`.** Fix the label or fix the oracle: extend `LADDER_HARNESS`'s string perturbations in `<repo>/src/synth/search/perturb.ts` to emit decimals, scientific notation, signs and padded whitespace, then re-score every arm. Corrected behavioural counts for v2 are **9/10 QuixBugs + 10/12 ladder = 19/22**.
- **Disclose that the ladder correctness oracle is the engine's own guard probe and is saturated.** `ladder-verdicts.mts:48` imports `LADDER_HARNESS`; `--seed 424242 --per-fn-cap 400` changes nothing. An oracle that shares its input catalogue with the thing it audits cannot bound generalisation.
- **Stop printing SWE "correct 6/6, overfit 0" as a correctness figure.** `<repo>/src/bench/headtohead.ts:92` hard-codes `correct = passes, overfit = 0` for swebench.
- **Label the dollar figures.** `$0.0986` of Jev cost is `costBasis: "table"` on 482/482 rows (rate-card, output free, no provider cost field exists); `$0.013914` of v2's `$0.045565` generator cost is stream-metered estimate across 37 of 100 rows, calibrated only by a 3-sample cancellation probe. `jev-off`'s `$0.506` is 100 % provider-reported. Say so beside the totals.
- **Pair the wall headline with a censoring-aware statistic.** 10 of 28 `jev-off` runs are wall-capped and 3 of those already passed; the both-solved medians are 35.1 s vs 111.8 s (v2/`jev-off`) and 39.5 s vs 92.0 s (v2/tuned, but median per-task ratio 0.842, v2 slower on 9/22).
- **Disclose the SWE selection mechanism.** The 6 instances are 6 of the 9 issue-oracle-VALID instances of a 30-instance pool; the 3 valid instances dropped are exactly the 3 the synthesizer never solved (0/3), while the 6 kept are 4/6.
- **Disclose the development-set provenance next to "28/28".** The report already concedes n is "a quarter to a fifth of the §10.3 design" and that no repeat run, no `LLM_GRACE_MS=0` run, no per-question ablation and no `llm-sieve` arm exist; the compressed claim carries none of that, and the guard constants and two template capabilities were fitted to instances inside the scored 28.
- **Resolve the untracked leftovers.** `bench/results/llm-jev-v2-leftovers-{detect-cycle,stats}` are post-bench re-fits of the two flagged tasks from an uncommitted tree; either remove them or check them in with provenance.

## Plain statement

Every number in the two claims reproduces exactly from the raw records — pass 28/28 vs 19/28 vs 22/28 on an identical 28-task set, paired discordance 9/0 and 6/0, nearest-rank medians 62.97/390.84/111.45 s, totals $0.144138/$0.506224/$0.242607, 482 Jev requests — the three committed head-to-head tool outputs and all six `verdicts.md` regenerate byte-identically, no patch touches a test file, the SWE evaluator resets tests to base plus the official `test_patch` and all six instances are fully green on FAIL_TO_PASS and PASS_TO_PASS, the task fixtures and suite evaluators are byte-identical between the two builds involved, and both worktrees have a single reflog entry and never moved. So what is verified is narrow and real: **on these 28 development tasks, at this build, the Jev-assisted search solved 9 tasks the untuned generator-only arm could not and 6 the hygiene-tuned one could not, lost none on pass, and did it at roughly a third of the tuned arm's per-task wall on the tasks both solved and about 60 % of its dollars — with the dollar figure part rate-card and part stream-estimate.** What is *not* verified is any generalisation: the 28 tasks are the same ones the guard's decision thresholds, two synthesis templates and the SWE instance list were fitted against, no llm-jev run has ever executed outside them, the ladder correctness oracle is the engine's own commit-guard probe and is provably saturated (it misses a real gold divergence in `units`, making the behavioural count 19/22 rather than 20/22), SWE "correctness" is defined as pass in code, and the 19/28 baseline is a different build measured eight hours earlier with no `generation` hygiene at all — for which the same-build control is 22/28, not 19/28.
